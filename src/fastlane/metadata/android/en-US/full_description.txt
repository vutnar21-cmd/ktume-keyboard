Kĕsje Tüme / kTüme (Кӗсье Тӳме / кТӳме) is a privacy-first, 100% offline customizable Android keyboard designed for the Chuvash language alongside Russian, Latin (Latina), and Greek layouts. It requests zero permissions (no internet access, no cloud auto-backup) and contains zero analytics, ads, or third-party cloud SDKs.

Key features:
- Two Chuvash layout presets: Balanced 26-key (10 + 9 + 7, keeping wide keys with native ӑ, ӗ, ҫ, ӳ on the primary layer) and Classic Russian ЙЦУКЕН + ӑ, ӗ, ҫ, ӳ (12 + 12 + 11 = 35 keys).
- Quick swipe-up gesture and slide-to-select long-press popups for secondary characters and punctuation.
- Built-in offline autocorrect and proper-name capitalization for Chuvash and Russian (160,000-word dictionaries bundled on-device) with single-tap Backspace undo and an on-device personal dictionary.
- Single-row top toolbar with cursor arrows, emoji picker, and one-tap clipboard paste (Latin lookalikes ăĕçÿ inside Cyrillic words become Chuvash ӑӗҫӳ without touching Latin text or passwords).
- Seamless key hitboxes, interactive row customizer, context-aware Enter action, automatic 3x4 Numpad for numeric/phone fields, full Unicode grapheme backspace, and 7 color palettes (including OLED dark).
